# Lunar New Year

**When:** Late January – February (moves each year)

## Project: Dragon Dance  *(planned)*

**The symbol:** The dragon dance winding through the streets.

**What you'll code:** Arrays that follow: each body segment moves to where the one in front was (the same trick as Snake).

**The printable:** Colour the dragon's head and design its scales, then plan its path on a grid.
