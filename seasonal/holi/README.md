# Holi

**When:** March (moves each year)

## Project: Colour Splash  *(planned)*

**The symbol:** The festival of colours: clouds of bright powder paint.

**What you'll code:** Colours and randomness: mixing your own colours with C_RGB and splatting them at random.

**The printable:** Mix colours on paper and write down the recipe (red, green, blue) for each.
