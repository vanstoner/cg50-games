# Rainy Day Summer

**When:** June – August

## Project: Raindrop Catcher  *(planned)*

**The symbol:** Stuck inside on a rainy summer day? Make a game!

**What you'll code:** Falling objects and collision: catch raindrops in a bucket before they hit the ground.

**The printable:** Design the bucket and the raindrop sprites, and invent the scoring rules.
