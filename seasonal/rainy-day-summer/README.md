# Rainy Day Summer

**When:** June – August

Stuck inside on a rainy summer day? Make a game!

## Project: [Rainy Day Dice](rainy-day-dice/)  ✅ Ready (micro:bit)

**What you'll code:** A micro:bit becomes five dice. Shake to roll, hold the dice you want with A and B, and get three rolls a turn. You score on a paper score sheet, so the whole family can play. Teaches random numbers, lists and True/False values.

**The printable:** Side 1 has how to play, how to read the dice on the 5 × 5 lights, and a score sheet for 2 to 4 players. Side 2 has the code with numbered notes.
