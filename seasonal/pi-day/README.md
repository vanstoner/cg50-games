# Pi Day

**When:** 14 March (3/14)

## Project: Raining Pi  *(planned)*

**The symbol:** π ≈ 3.14, and you can find it by throwing darts!

**What you'll code:** Random numbers and maths: drop random dots on a square, count how many land in the circle, and watch π appear.

**The printable:** Do it by hand first: scatter rice on a printed circle-in-a-square and count.
