# Mother's Day

**When:** UK: March (moves each year) · US and many others: May

## Project: Flower for Mum  *(planned)*

**The symbol:** A flower and a message for someone special.

**What you'll code:** Loops and angles: draw petals around a circle, then let the flower grow and bloom.

**The printable:** Design the flower and write a message to show on the calculator.
