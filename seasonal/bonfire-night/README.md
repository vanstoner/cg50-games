# Bonfire Night

**When:** 5 November (UK)

## Project: Firework Show  *(planned)*

**The symbol:** Fireworks lighting up the November sky.

**What you'll code:** Particles and gravity: hundreds of sparks, each with its own speed, falling back to earth. A real physics simulation!

**The printable:** Draw how a firework moves over time, and invent your own firework types.
