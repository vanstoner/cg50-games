# Ramadan

**When:** Moves each year (Islamic lunar calendar)

## Project: Crescent Moon Tracker  *(planned)*

**The symbol:** The crescent moon that marks the start and end of the month, and the lanterns (fanous) that light the evenings.

**What you'll code:** Drawing with circles: make every phase of the moon by overlapping two circles, and step through the month day by day.

**The printable:** Colour the moon phases for a month and design a pixel lantern.
